Glove

Material
sythetic leather, foam
Manufacturer
Fairtex

The objects appear to be protective coverings designed for human hands. Scanning reveals thick layers of padding, suggesting that they were made to absorb physical impact. Historical records associate similar objects with a sport known as boxing, in which participants repeatedly struck one another using their fists. The purpose of such intense physical contact remains unclear in a society where physical combat can be simulated through neural systems. Researchers suggest that the activity may have been practiced for competition or physical training.
