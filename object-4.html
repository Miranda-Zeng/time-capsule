COLLECTION 006 / ITEM 04

Wire Tool

Material
plastic, copper wire, metal
Manufacturer
unknown

The object consists of two small devices connected by wires. Its shape suggests that the devices were designed to be placed inside human ears. Researchers hypothesize that it may have been an early attempt at connecting the human nervous system to electronic devices, possibly for transmitting information through the body. Further examination reveals that the connector fits into a port on the vintage iPhone found in the same suitcase. This raises another possibility: maybe the device have been designed to transfer energy from the human body to power the phone?
