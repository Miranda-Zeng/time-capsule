COLLECTION 006 / ITEM 02

Plastic Human Figure

Material
plastic, magnet
Origin
likely Berlin

The objects feature two small humanoid figures in red and green, with magnetic backing. Visual recognition and historical records suggest that these figures originated from pedestrian traffic signals in East Berlin, designed in 1961. They were later adopted as cultural symbols of the city. Given their small size, magnetic backing, and connection to traffic signals, researchers hypothesize that they may have been used as educational tools to teach children basic traffic rules.
